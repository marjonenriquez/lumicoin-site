About The Atlas

v1.2

Translating biological measurements into a computable map of cellular space.

What is it?

A mathematically constrained visual atlas built from stereology and volumetric evidence. Each cell is represented by 1,000 particles, where 100% equals that cell’s internal volume budget.

Who is it for?

Built for students, researchers, and curious minds who want to compare how different human cells allocate internal space.

The Origin

My name is Marjon Enriquez — a systems bioenergetics researcher who first put this together to see proportions of mitochondria across cell types based on the most recent evidence. Then I expanded it to the nucleus. Then I thought, why not expand it to all organelles! Hence this atlas.
